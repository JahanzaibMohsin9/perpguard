PerpGuard shows how close your Hyperliquid perpetual position is to liquidation, right on your G2 lens, and lets you protect it in seconds.

On the glasses:
- One card per position: side, leverage, mark price, liquidation price and a distance bar with SAFE / WARNING / DANGER.
- "~N min to liq" when the price is moving toward your liquidation.
- Tap for protective actions only: close 50%, close all, stop at break-even, or a 2% stop. Every action needs a second tap to confirm.
- Hold to talk: say "close half" or "stop at break even". Speech-to-text runs on your own PerpGuard server.

With Telegram:
- Alerts when a position crosses into WARNING or DANGER, with one-tap protective buttons and receipts.
- A Mini App with live cards, and voice notes in groups.
- Optional Guardian mode: if a DANGER alert goes unanswered, it closes a small part of the position and places a safety stop.

Safety:
- Testnet only. PerpGuard uses a Hyperliquid API (agent) wallet, which can trade but can never withdraw funds.
- It never opens or increases a position.
- Packaged glasses pair with your bot using a one-time 6-digit code.

PerpGuard needs your own PerpGuard server (open source): https://github.com/jahanzaibmohsin9/perpguard
This is a risk tool, not financial advice.
